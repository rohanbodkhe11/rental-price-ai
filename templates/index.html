PROBLEM & PURPOSE

Why Student Performance Prediction?

Academic performance is influenced by multiple factors such as attendance, study habits, previous academic performance, assessment scores, sleep patterns, and academic workload. This project uses machine learning to identify patterns in these factors and estimate a student's expected final percentage.

Academic Insights

Understand how key academic indicators relate directly to overall semester performance.

Behavioral Factors

Analyze study time, class attendance, and lifestyle-related habits to uncover core habits.

Actionable Feedback

Receive personalized suggestions tailored to improve consistency and overall outcomes.

INPUT PARAMETERS

What Does the Model Analyze?

The ML model evaluates nine distinct academic, attendance, and lifestyle indicators.

Study Hours

Average focused study time per day.

Attendance

Percentage of classes attended during the semester.

Previous Performance

Overall academic percentage from the previous semester.

Assignment Score

Average score across coursework and assignments.

Internal Test Score

Performance in internal and mid-term assessments.

Sleep Hours

Daily sleep duration for cognitive recovery and focus.

Extracurricular Activities

Participation in co-curricular activities.

Internet Access

Availability of high-speed study resources online.

Backlogs

Count of pending or uncleared active backlogs.
